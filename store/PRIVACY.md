# Toast Docs privacy policy

Toast Docs works on your computer. Your files stay on your computer.

**What Toast Docs does not do**
- It has no account and asks for no personal information.
- It does not collect, sell or share your files, your data or how you use the app.
- It has no ads and no tracking.

**When something goes over the internet (only if you use it)**
- **Built-in AI:** runs on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. (The Lite version downloads the AI once from our GitHub page.)
- **Online AI (optional, off unless you add your own key):** if you add a key for Google Gemini, ChatGPT, Grok or Claude and press Ask, the file you ask about is sent to that company to read and answer, under that company's privacy policy. Your key is kept only on your computer.
- **Translate:** the text of the pages you translate is sent to Google Translate.
- **Updates (not the Microsoft Store version):** the app checks our GitHub releases page for a newer version.
- **Problem report:** saved as a file on your Desktop. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/usernam8/MyPDFKit-releases/issues
